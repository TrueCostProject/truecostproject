Petco, prove it.

Cut ties with Marshall BioResources.

The ask

Petco publicly commits to stop doing business with Marshall BioResources. That means pulling Marshall Pet Products from Petco.com right away and no more restocking Marshall products or Marshall-bred ferrets in stores.

Why Petco

Petco tells its customers that animals always come first. Marshall BioResources breeds dogs and other animals to be sold into laboratories. Petco can't claim one while it does business with the other.

True Cost Project helped put beagle testing in the national spotlight. Chewy cut ties with Marshall soon after, and PetSmart followed once our protests hit its stores. Petco is the last major holdout, and it's out of excuses.

Action guidelines

  • Stay nonviolent and stay legal. No exceptions.
  • Park off Petco property.
  • Every store has animals inside, including the companions who come in with their families. Keep chants and megaphones well away from the doors.
  • Hand out flyers and talk with shoppers. Plenty of them have never heard of Marshall.
  • No animal products at any protest. That covers anything you wear, eat or bring.
  • Non-vegans are welcome, and we encourage you to bring them. Standing with the animals can be what gets someone to go vegan, so invite them to a vegan restaurant with you afterward. Each city organizer decides what works for their group.
  • We have a call-in, not call-out, policy. If something feels off, talk to the person directly and kindly, or bring it to an organizer. No public shaming.
  • Film your action and upload the footage using the Upload footage button on this page.
  • Check with your organizer before posting protest times, places or anyone's face.
  • Tag every post #PetcoProveIt.
