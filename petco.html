Petco, prove it.

Cut ties with Marshall BioResources.

The ask

Petco makes a written or public promise to cut ties with Marshall BioResources and Marshall Pet Products. That means taking Marshall Pet Products off Petco.com right away and bringing in nothing new from Marshall, including Marshall-bred ferrets. Petco can sell what's already on its shelves, and the ferrets already in its stores can still go to loving homes.

Why Petco

Petco tells its customers that animals always come first. Marshall BioResources breeds dogs and other animals to be sold into laboratories. Petco can't claim one while it does business with the other.

True Cost Project helped put beagle testing in the national spotlight. Chewy cut ties with Marshall soon after, and PetSmart followed once our protests hit its stores. Petco is the last major holdout, and it's out of excuses.

Before the protest

Get everyone together for a quick huddle before you start. Go over who's doing what, do a short practice run, and answer any questions.

Leave bags and extras in your car and bring as little as possible.

How a protest works

Every protest has two teams.

The outreach team stands near the store entrance. They:

  • Hand out leaflets.
  • Talk with customers.
  • Show footage from inside the labs on a TV, iPad or laptop. This is one of the most important parts of the protest. Most people have never seen what happens to these animals, and the footage changes minds. Every city needs at least one screen.

The loud team stands farther away, on the public sidewalk. They:

  • Hold signs.
  • Lead chants with megaphones.
  • Give speak-outs.

Why two teams? There are animals inside every Petco, and customers often bring their companion animals with them. We never want to frighten them. So the loud team stays far from the entrance, and no one uses megaphones inside or near the door.

Indoor speak-outs

An indoor speak-out is a short speech given inside the store. It's calm, not loud.

  • Use your normal speaking voice. No yelling.
  • Keep it to 1 to 2 minutes.
  • Hand the store manager a letter. Use the template below and fill it in with your own words.
  • Then leave.
  • Always have someone film the whole speak-out, start to finish.

Letter template

Hi [store manager's name, or "Hello"],

My name is [your name], and I live here in [city]. I'm reaching out as part of Petco Prove It, a national campaign led by True Cost Project. Please pass this message on to Petco corporate.

[One or two sentences in your own words about why this matters to you.]

Petco says "animals first," but it still sells Marshall Pet Products and ferrets bred by Marshall. Marshall Pet Products is part of the same family of companies as Marshall BioResources, which breeds animals for laboratory testing. Chewy and PetSmart have already cut ties with Marshall.

We're asking Petco to make a public commitment to cut ties with Marshall BioResources and Marshall Pet Products.

If corporate would like to respond, they can reach the campaign at proveit@truecostproject.org.

Thank you,
[your name]

Private property

  • The store and its parking lot belong to Petco. If staff ask you to leave, leave calmly and join the group on the public sidewalk.
  • Park off Petco's property.

Staying safe

If someone yells at the protest team, don't engage. Let the outreach team handle conversations.

If police show up, point them to your police liaison without saying anyone's name. Nobody else talks to them.

If someone grabs your sign, let them have it. If someone touches you, say "Do not touch me" and step back.

We keep everything peaceful and legal, but nobody can guarantee you won't be cited or arrested. If you want the lowest-risk way to help, stick with the outreach team outside.

Filming: this matters a lot

Videos are how millions of people will hear about this campaign. Good footage gets shared and gets pushed by Instagram and TikTok. Please make filming a priority.

  • Hold your phone up and down (vertical), like you're taking a selfie.
  • Hold it steady. Use both hands or lean on something.
  • Get close to whoever is speaking so we can hear them clearly.
  • Film the signs, the crowd and the Petco sign so viewers know where you are.
  • Film the indoor speak-out from start to finish.
  • If staff or police come over, keep recording calmly.

Roles

You don't have to take on any role. Just showing up and holding a sign helps a lot, and it's a great way to start.

  • Sign holder: hold a sign on the sidewalk.
  • Leafleter: hand out flyers and talk with customers.
  • Footage screen: set up the TV, iPad or laptop and keep the footage playing.
  • Videographer: film the protest, following the filming tips above.
  • Speaker: lead chants or give a speak-out.
  • Parking and location: check out the store ahead of time and tell everyone where to park.
  • Police liaison: the one person who talks to police if they show up. This should be someone who has done a know your rights training.

Ground rules

  • Everything we do is peaceful and legal.
  • Don't wear or bring any animal products.
  • Be kind to each other. If someone makes a mistake, talk to them privately.
  • Invite friends and family. These are low-key protests and everyone is welcome.
  • Know your rights before you go.
  • When you talk to customers, stick to the facts on our flyers and talking points. They're powerful enough on their own. If someone asks something you don't know, it's fine to say "I'm not sure, but I can find out."
