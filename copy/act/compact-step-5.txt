Keep it calm and friendly. Filming is generally legal in publicly accessible areas like lobbies and hallways, but not inside private office space without permission. Keep your phone out where everyone can see it, and if staff ask you to stop, stop. Then politely ask permission to record the conversation in good faith, for transparency. The goal is to show your rep in a good light to the world and to their constituents. You can even offer to keep the camera on yourself only.
