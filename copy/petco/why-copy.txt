Petco tells its customers that animals always come first. Marshall BioResources breeds dogs and other animals to be sold into laboratories. Petco can't claim one while it does business with the other.

Chewy and PetSmart already cut ties with Marshall after True Cost Project and other organizations brought them the evidence and applied pressure. Nothing bad happened to either of them. Petco is the last major holdout, and it's out of excuses.
