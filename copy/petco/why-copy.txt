Petco tells its customers that animals always come first. Marshall BioResources breeds dogs and other animals to be sold into laboratories. Petco can't claim one while it does business with the other.

True Cost Project helped put beagle testing in the national spotlight. Chewy cut ties with Marshall soon after, and PetSmart followed once our protests hit its stores. Petco is the last major holdout, and it's out of excuses.
