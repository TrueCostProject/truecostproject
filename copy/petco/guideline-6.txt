Non-vegans are welcome, and we encourage you to bring them. At Ridglan, we watched hundreds of people who weren't vegan go vegan after fighting for the dogs. Invite them to a vegan restaurant with you afterward. We also understand some people want a safe space, so each city organizer decides what works for their group. Over time, we do expect the people who keep showing up to go vegan and respect the lives of all animals.
