Tell Petco: cut ties with Marshall BioResources

Chewy and PetSmart have cut ties with Marshall BioResources, the lab animal breeder that sells dogs, ferrets, and other animals to laboratories for live experimentation. Petco says it puts animals first. We're asking Petco to prove it.

We call on Petco to stop selling Marshall Pet Products and Marshall-bred ferrets, and to cut ties with Marshall BioResources.

Whistleblower evidence submitted to Wayne County, New York authorities on August 31, 2026 alleges that dogs at Marshall were killed painfully, including one stomped on the neck and injected with a lethal drug in his belly because staff couldn't find a vein, and that healthy dogs were killed over things like eye color. A former federal prosecutor's criminal referral says the evidence warrants an investigation into potential felony animal cruelty. These are allegations, and Marshall denies them. Petco shouldn't be doing business with a company facing them.
